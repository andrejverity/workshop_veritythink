Project 10 · Run on request

Scholarship Hunter.

Funding for study, found from a profile in a spreadsheet. The search runs wide, from the university to the country to the funders behind a nationality, and it shows its work: what fits, what does not, and where nobody could say.

InputOne row per applicant
OutputShortlist and exclusions
RunsOn request

How the sheet is laid out

The structure, minus the spreadsheet itself: the applicants and the findings stay private.

  1. Applicants.One row per person: what and where they plan to study, entry year, citizenships and place of residence, qualifications and predicted grades, age, whether financial need applies, and any leadership record.
  2. Relevant scholarships.The shortlist, each with its amount, deadline, conditions and a link to the source. Leads that still hinge on an open question are marked as such.
  3. Not relevant.Everything checked and ruled out, with the reason. A scholarship that needs financial need is not quietly dropped, it is logged as excluded and why.
  4. Scan coverage.A dated list of every kind of source checked for each applicant, so anyone can see how complete a search was and where it stopped.

How a scan works

  1. Read the profile.The row sets the filters. Missing details are left blank and flagged rather than guessed.
  2. Search in layers.What each target university offers. What the study country offers to international students. What the home countries and regions offer to their own citizens. Then independent funders, foundations and clubs, and public student loans.
  3. Read the rules, not the headline.Eligibility pages are read for residence tests, grade cutoffs, subject requirements and deadlines. Where a rule is unclear for a particular situation, the answer is a question to put to the awarding body, not a guess.
  4. Log and report.Findings go into the workbook, and a short summary goes to the person who asked.

Where it stands

It has been tested on profile-driven searches: details go into the sheet, a scan runs, and it is re-run as missing details are filled in. A few checks, such as a university publishing its terms for next year, are repeated on a watch. There is no fixed schedule: a scan runs when it is asked for.

House rules

  • Researches and reports only. It does not apply, email awarding bodies, or pay for anything without being asked.
  • Every entry carries its source. Amounts and deadlines are as published and worth confirming before anyone relies on them.
  • Uncertain eligibility is called uncertain. Nothing is presented as a confirmed award.
  • Names, profiles, grades and findings are private. This page describes the machine, not the people.
