Project 05 · Runs on a weekly rhythm

Sale scanning.

A shopping list that checks the shops itself. The list lives in a simple spreadsheet; the scan reads every store page fresh and reports only genuine sales. When nothing is cheaper, nothing is said. Silence is the feature.

CadenceWeekly
False alarmsNot tolerated
Promo dialectsDutch, fluent

How it works

The setup, minus the spreadsheet itself: the list stays private.

  1. The list.Products, stores and the price each item cost when it went on the list, one row each. Adding a row is the whole onboarding process; the next scan picks it up.
  2. The rounds.On schedule, every store page is read live: the current price, and the promotion mechanics Dutch shops love: "1+1 gratis", "2e halve prijs", kassakorting and friends.
  3. The judgement.A crossed-out price on a store page is marketing, not evidence. The reference price in the list is the bar, and only a real drop or a confirmed, still-running promotion clears it.
  4. The message.One compact digest of what is actually on sale, with price, promo text and link. Each row chooses where its news goes.

House rules

  • On sale means on sale: a verified price drop or an active promotion, checked on the live page on the day.
  • Not on sale means not mentioned. A digest of non-events is just noise with a subject line.
  • A store having a bad technical day earns one line at the bottom, not a morning of retries.
  • The list itself is private. This page describes the machine, not the shopping.
