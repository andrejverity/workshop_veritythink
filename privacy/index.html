All projects

The pages in The Workshop are static pages on GitHub Pages. They set no cookies of their own and use no advertising or analytics trackers of their own. As with any website, the hosting service may log technical details such as your IP address in the ordinary way. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Some projects read public data or use a Google Sheet or a Google Apps Script web app behind the page. Any project that collects information from visitors says so in its own section below.

To ask a question or request deletion, use the contact page on veritythink.com.

Reading the board

Anyone can read the notes without signing in. Reading collects nothing about you from this page. As with any website, the hosting services (GitHub Pages and Google Apps Script) may log technical details such as your IP address in the ordinary way.

What Google sign-in collects

Signing in is only for people who want to edit. When you click "Sign in with Google", Google tells this page your email address, your name and your profile picture. That is all. The page asks for no other access to your Google account: no Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.

What is stored, and where

Your email address is checked against an allow list kept in a private Google Sheet. If you are an approved editor, the sheet holds your email address, your role and an approval status. If you sign in without being approved, your email address may be added as "pending", with no edit rights, so the owner can decide whether to approve you. Notes you post are stored in the sheet with your email address and first name. The public view shows only the first name, never an email address. Your picture is not stored. The page keeps nothing in your browser after you close the tab, and it sets no cookies of its own.

Who can see it

Only Andrej Verity and the assistant that maintains the site for him can see the allow list and the email addresses in it. The notes themselves are public. Your information is used only to decide who may edit. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. The site runs on GitHub Pages and a Google Apps Script web app, which process requests on the owner's behalf.

Deleting your information

Send a request through the contact page below and your row is removed from the allow list, and any notes you posted can be removed on request. You can also remove this app's access under Google Account, Security, Third-party apps and services, though the app holds no access to your account beyond sign-in.
